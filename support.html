FER is free and will stay free. It's one person building roads in the hours left over after work, and a community keeping it alive with feedback, photos and donations. Here's how you can help.

Ways to Support

Pick whatever suits you. Or don't, and just drive.

  • Chip In Financially

    Mapping takes time, and time is the one thing this project runs out of fastest. Donations literally buy hours of development. Patreon or PayPal, whichever you prefer. And if you can't chip in, that's fine too. Playing and reporting bugs helps just as much. Find me on Patreon or PayPal.

  • Send Reference Material

    Live in Yakutia or somewhere out east? Your photos and dashcam clips are gold. Real streets, real signs, real bus stops, that's what makes cities here recognizable. Drop them in the Discord.

  • Spread the Word

    Stream a convoy, post screenshots, make a video, tell a friend. Word of mouth is the only marketing this map has ever had, and it works.

Where Your Support Goes

Development time. More hours at the editor means new cities and roads sooner. Simple as that.
